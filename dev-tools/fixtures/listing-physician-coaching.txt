WebsiteClosers® presents an SBA Pre-Qualified Physician Coaching Company helping doctors and advanced practice providers beat burnout and stay in the careers they worked so hard to build. This trusted brand has spent 16 years earning the confidence of a skeptical audience, teaching clinicians to set boundaries, let go of perfectionism, and finish their charts on time. Since 2023, annual sales have grown from about $800,000 to more than $3.4 million in 2025, with over 1,000 healthcare professionals coached and an NPS of 8.3 across their client base.

SBA Pre-Qualification

This business has been pre-qualified for SBA financing, offering a distinct advantage to prospective acquirers. For qualified buyers, this means the company meets stringent lender criteria for consistent cash flow, operational stability, and financial documentation, reducing uncertainty and expediting the lending process. With only 10% down required, buyers can secure ownership through a 10-year, fully amortized loan at competitive fixed interest rates, allowing for an affordable monthly payment structure and maximum leverage of capital.

Business Model

The business delivers a five-month, one-on-one coaching program for physicians, nurse practitioners, and physician assistants who are worn out and thinking about leaving medicine. Physicians pay $9,800, NPs and PAs pay $7,800, and clients who want more time can add a four-month extension at half the original price. Each client also gets five self-paced courses and group coaching clinics five times a week. The program earned CME certification in 2025, so clients can pay with their employer’s education funds. Hospitals and health systems buy through Coach on Record deals ($10,000 to $15,000+) that carry no sales commissions or ad spend, or larger packages ($55,000 to $65,000) that cost less than 10% of contract value to fulfill. One national healthcare partner raised physician retention to 93% after their first program and has since ordered five more. All payments are collected upfront, keeping the business cash flow positive every month with no working capital needs.

Digital Marketing & Lead Generation

Customer acquisition runs mainly through paid Meta ads managed by an outside agency. Two funnels feed the sales team. The first uses short-form video ads with native lead forms, and every lead automatically receives a text before a setter calls to book an appointment. The second promotes a live weekly masterclass, and attendees who book a call afterward receive a $1,000 discount. Together, these funnels produce 150 to 200 live sales calls a month and return 3x on ad spend. The core client is a female family or internal medicine physician aged 30 to 45, earning $200,000 to $300,000 a year, who wants more time with her family and more joy at work. TikTok ads are now in testing.

Operations

The company runs fully remote with low overhead and SOPs for onboarding, webinar setup, and community management. A team of 20+ associate coaches, all former physicians, nurse practitioners, or healthcare workers, delivers nearly every session, and ownership’s share of coaching sessions dropped from 19% in 2025 to just 5% in 2026. Coaches are hired against ICF standards and receive monthly mentoring. A Customer Success Manager onboards each new client and matches them with a coach. A Head of Operations manages daily tasks, while a sales manager leads four setters and four closers. Coaches never work inside a client’s EHR, which keeps the business clear of HIPAA concerns.

Business Broker Takeaways

1. Proven Team-Based Delivery. Clinician coaches handle 95% of sessions, and the brand, frameworks, and course library transfer with the sale, so a buyer does not need a medical background to run the business.

2. Established Brand Recognition. With a 16-year history and a strong reputation in the healthcare coaching industry, the brand is synonymous with quality and effectiveness. This recognition, along with proprietary intellectual property such as self-paced educational resources and a popular wellness book, strengthens its market position and appeal to potential buyers.

3. Strong Financial Performance. Sales grew 134% from 2024 to 2025, seller’s discretionary earnings rose 76% over the same period, and margins remain above 30%.

Growth Opportunities

Opportunities to scale are many. Physician burnout costs U.S. healthcare organizations an estimated $4.6 billion a year, and replacing one physician can cost more than $500,000, yet the company runs no B2B marketing today. A buyer could hire a B2B sales rep, run LinkedIn campaigns aimed at hospital leaders, and turn last year’s 500 added clients into introductions at their own employers. The CRM holds about 20,000 opted-in physicians who have never purchased, and two new SDRs are already seeing strong early results at zero ad spend. A formal referral program, leadership coaching at $15,000 to $20,000 per engagement, and new programs for nurses, dentists, and veterinarians on the 22,000-contact email list are all within reach. Licensing the coaching method to health systems and offering partner products to a loyal physician network represent untapped revenue streams.

This coaching brand’s established presence and strategic growth opportunities make it a compelling investment for those looking to make a significant impact in the healthcare industry.
